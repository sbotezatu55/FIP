Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Flight Intelligence Platform.

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Information FIP may collect

FIP may receive flight telemetry files that you choose to upload, along with basic technical information required to operate and protect the service, such as request details and error information.

Uploaded flight data

Uploaded data is used to import, validate, reconstruct, analyze, and display flight information. Do not upload confidential, sensitive, proprietary, or safety-critical flight data.

Cookies and analytics

The current application does not include configured analytics or advertising trackers. If this changes, this policy will be updated to describe the relevant technologies and choices.

Storage and third-party services

Imported flight information may be stored by the application for later retrieval and analysis. FIP uses its configured hosting, database, and infrastructure services to operate the application. OpenSky is the current flight-data source represented by the import workflow.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect application data, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Avoid uploading information that requires a higher level of protection.

Your choices

You can choose not to upload data, and you can contact the project owner with questions about information associated with your use of FIP. Requests may be limited by available records and applicable obligations.
