AI & Advanced Flight Analysis

Artificial intelligence is being explored as an analytical layer for FIP—not as a replacement for deterministic aviation calculations. Core facts such as aircraft position, altitude, speed, calculated distances, and detected events should remain based on traceable data and algorithms.

AI may eventually assist with flight summarization, explanation of unusual flight behavior, pattern identification, flight comparison, interpretation of reconstructed operational sequences, and correlation between telemetry and aviation context.

The objective is to move beyond “Where did the aircraft fly?” toward “What happened during the flight, and what aviation context helps explain it?”
