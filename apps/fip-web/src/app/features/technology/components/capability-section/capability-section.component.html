Flight Reconstruction

Telemetry observations are processed chronologically to reconstruct aircraft movement through time and space.

The reconstruction layer evaluates position, altitude, groundspeed, vertical movement, timing, and continuity of available telemetry.

Flight Event Detection

Raw telemetry becomes significantly more useful when it can be interpreted in aviation terms.

  • Takeoff
  • Landing
  • Top of Climb
  • Top of Descent
  • Telemetry Gaps

Geospatial Analysis

Aircraft movement is inherently geospatial. FIP uses geographic calculations to analyze aircraft positions, distances, trajectories, and movement between telemetry observations.
